Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upload my music? +
Head to the Upload page from the navigation bar. Fill in your artist details, select your genre and language, then attach your audio file and submit. Your track will be reviewed within 48 hours.
Is BEE MUSIC free for artists? +
Yes — BEE MUSIC is completely free for independent artists to register and upload. We believe talent shouldn't be gated by fees. Our mission is to give every voice a platform.
What audio formats are supported? +
We support MP3, WAV, FLAC, and AAC formats. For the best streaming quality, we recommend uploading in WAV or FLAC at 44.1 kHz / 16-bit or higher.
How are featured artists selected? +
Our editorial team reviews stream counts, audience engagement, and overall track quality each month. The artist with the most consistent growth and listener interaction earns the Featured spot.
Can listeners leave feedback? +
Absolutely. Listeners can like artists and tracks. We are actively building a full comments and rating system which will roll out in the next platform update.
How do I report a technical issue? +
Use the contact form above, select "Report a Problem" as the subject, and describe the issue in detail. Our team typically responds within 24 hours on business days.
